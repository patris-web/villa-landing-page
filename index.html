Welcome

Welcome to our little paradise made for you

Villa Broke is imagined as a place where modern comfort meets the quiet elegance of island living. Everything here is designed to slow the pace down and let the setting speak for itself.

From the first morning light over the sea to long afternoons by the pool and soft evenings under the stars, the villa offers an atmosphere of privacy, warmth and understated sophistication. Clean lines, natural tones, open views and curated interiors shape an experience that feels both luxurious and deeply relaxed.

Whether you are visiting for a romantic getaway, a family holiday or a special celebration with friends, this is a destination made for meaningful moments and effortless comfort.

About the Experience

Our Story

Built around atmosphere, openness and calm

The vision behind Villa Broke was simple: create a residence that feels exclusive without ever feeling excessive.

Inspired by Maldivean textures, contemporary architecture and the emotional quality of open space, the villa embraces the idea that true luxury is not about accumulation, but about clarity, balance and ease. Every detail, from the flow of the rooms to the placement of each outdoor area, is centered around comfort, light and connection to the landscape.

It is a home designed for slow breakfasts, private swims, sunset conversations and evenings that stretch beautifully into the night. Elegant, grounded and welcoming, Villa Broke invites guests to experience luxury in its most natural form.

Fine Dine

From beautifully styled breakfasts to sunset aperitifs and private dinner moments, the culinary side of the villa extends the same effortless elegance found in every other detail of the stay.

Location

Placed where panoramic views and privacy meet

Perfectly positioned to offer both seclusion and access, Villa Broke lets guests enjoy the peaceful side of the island while remaining close to its most desirable points of interest.

The setting is ideal for those who want to move between quiet luxury and lively island energy with ease. Spend the day exploring beaches, hidden corners and elegant dining spots, then return to a private sanctuary defined by stillness, comfort and expansive views.

This is a location that gives you the best of both worlds: connection when you want it, calm when you need it and beauty in every direction.
